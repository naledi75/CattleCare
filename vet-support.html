Frequently Asked Questions

How do I review a health case?

Open Health Cases from the sidebar and select View Case. You can review symptoms, the AI preliminary assessment and submit your veterinary diagnosis.

How does the AI assessment work?

CattleCare provides a preliminary screening based on the information submitted by the farmer. The result is intended to support veterinary review and does not replace professional veterinary diagnosis.

How do I manage appointments?

Open Appointments from the sidebar to review and manage farmer appointment requests.
